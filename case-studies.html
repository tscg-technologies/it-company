DEMO CASE STUDY 01 // ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIO

E-Commerce Operations & Inventory Modernization

The Business Challenge

A multi-warehouse retail distributor faced constant inventory synchronization delays between online storefronts and physical fulfillment centers, resulting in overselling and manual order cancellations.

The Proposed Solution

Engineered an event-driven inventory middleware service utilizing WebSocket queues and atomic database locks to reserve stock in sub-second intervals across channels.

Architecture Overview

Decoupled microservice with Redis cache locks, PostgreSQL transactional storage, and webhooks syncing with Shopify and ERP backends.

Technology Stack (Illustrative)
Node.js PostgreSQL Redis Docker

DEMO CASE STUDY 02 // ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIO

Business Reporting Automation & Centralized BI

The Business Challenge

An international professional services group spent dozens of manual analyst hours every week copying spreadsheet columns between CRM, billing, and time-tracking systems to produce executive summaries.

The Proposed Solution

Built an automated nightly ETL ingestion pipeline consolidating billing and operations data into a normalized star-schema warehouse feeding an interactive executive dashboard.

Architecture Overview

Automated Python ETL workers, Snowflake/BigQuery staging schemas, and Power BI embedded visualization models.

Technology Stack (Illustrative)
Python Power BI SQL Cron Pipelines
Related Services

Data Analytics & BI, AI & Automation, and IT Consulting.

DEMO CASE STUDY 03 // ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIO

Customer Support Workflow & Intent Escalation

The Business Challenge

A SaaS enterprise platform experienced ticket backlog spikes following product updates, with urgent account access issues languishing in the same general queue as minor cosmetic suggestions.

The Proposed Solution

Constructed an intelligent natural language ticket parsing engine that auto-labels incoming tickets by urgency, matches account health tiers, and drafts recommended replies for human review.

Architecture Overview

Webhook ingest gateway, NLP vector embedding classifier, deterministic SLA queue, and Zendesk/HubSpot API sync.

Technology Stack (Illustrative)
NLP / AI Node.js REST APIs Webhooks
Related Services

AI Solutions, AI & Automation, and Digital Transformation.
